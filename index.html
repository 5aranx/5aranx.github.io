Flagship builds

01

PUF Drone Authentication

A drone that proves who it is from its silicon.

A UAV authentication system built on Physical Unclonable Functions (PUFs), so each drone is identified from its silicon rather than from a stored credential. Keys are derived on demand from the PUF response, which means no secret key is kept on the device - nothing at rest to extract or clone.

PUFUAVHardware SecurityKey Derivation

02

Mayavi

One ESP32-S3 that is a keyboard, mouse, gamepad and media remote.

ESP32-S3 firmware that acts as a Bluetooth LE HID device - keyboard, mouse, gamepad and media remote - with 11 selectable profiles, plus a Wi-Fi access point with a browser dashboard and a serial console. A non-blocking payload runner sits on NVS-backed settings and a LittleFS-hosted JSON API. Authorized-use limits and security trade-offs, such as the open access point default and Just Works pairing, are documented.
